# Camera vocabulary (EN | 中文) — use exactly one per shot

| Move | Prompt wording | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Push in / dolly in | slow dolly-in toward the face | 缓慢推镜 |
| Pull out | dolly-out revealing the whole room | 拉镜 |
| Pan | smooth pan left to right, tripod fixed | 摇镜（水平） |
| Tilt | tilt up from boots to face | 俯仰摇 |
| Track / follow | tracking shot following behind at walking pace | 跟拍 |
| Orbit | 180-degree orbit around the subject | 环绕 |
| Crane / jib | crane up and back to a wide | 升降镜头 |
| Handheld | handheld, subtle shake | 手持 |
| Whip pan | whip pan to the next subject | 甩镜 |
| Dolly zoom | dolly zoom (vertigo) on the actor | 希区柯克变焦 |
| FPV / drone | FPV drone dive through the gap | 穿越机 |
| Static | locked-off static camera | 固定镜头 |
| One take | one continuous take, no cuts | 一镜到底 |

Shot sizes: extreme wide, wide, medium, medium close-up, close-up, extreme close-up, over-the-shoulder, low angle, high angle, top-down.
Avoid stacking (orbit + zoom + tilt); avoid "dynamic camera" (unspecific). To copy a move: `follow all camera movement from @video1`.
